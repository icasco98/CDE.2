# The known house

A built private villa in Al-Salam, Hawalli governorate, plot 274 in
block 6, from the consultant's Municipality set of 24 March 2025
(area sheets and floor plans; the owner supplied them for O3). Names
are left out. Every number below is read off the drawings; where the
drawing and the Municipality page can be compared, the house confirms
the page.

## What it says for the tool

- The extended-family villa: the basement is the reception floor
  (diwaniya, dining, service), the ground floor is the parents' floor,
  the first and second are each a son's flat with its own living,
  dining, pantry and suites. The program repeats by storey.
- The stair and lift stand at one spot through five floors, off the
  entrance, in the middle of the south half. Every floor's lobby is
  4.60 × 1.40 beside them.
- Bedrooms are 16 to 29 m², living rooms 35 to 45 m², dining 26 to 49
  m², kitchen 25 m², maid 7 m², driver 6 m², WCs 3 to 4 m². These sit
  inside the ranges of `room-types.md` except the basement dining and
  buffet, which together are larger than the table's top.
- Every bedroom has its bath; the master suites have a dressing room
  as well. The tool's companion rule (ensuite per bedroom) matches;
  a dressing room per master suite is not yet in the table.
- No garage on the plot at all. The garage placeholder on the ground
  floor (decision 20, B0) is one choice; this house makes the other.
- The service street is the only street, so the entrance, the sunken
  court and the balcony all face it; the privacy gradient runs west
  from the street.
