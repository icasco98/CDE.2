# The fresh brief

The brief for the usability run (O5), written by the owner with the
cofounder on 13 September 2026, in the words a client uses. It is a
different house from the known house on purpose: a corner plot, two
streets, cars on the setback, no basement and no second floor yet, so
the run tests choices the known house did not make. Size words map to
the room-type ranges: big is the top of the kind's range, medium the
middle, small the bottom.

**Client.** Parents, three sons aged 12 to 20, one daughter, a maid
and a driver. The eldest son marries within five years and wants his
own flat upstairs.

**Plot.** Corner plot in Sabah Al-Salem, 500 m², 20 m on the service
street to the south, 25 m on the side street to the east. Flat.
Neighbours west and north.

**Storeys.** Ground and first. Second floor later, when the son
marries.

**Ground.** A big diwaniya at the corner with its own door from the
street, a WC and a small kitchen for it. Entry. A big family living. A
medium formal living. Dining for twelve. A medium kitchen with a
pantry. Guest WC. The maid's and driver's rooms with their baths, off
the service side. A store. A small garden on the west.

**First.** The parents' bedroom, big, with a dressing room and a bath.
Three bedrooms, medium, each with its bath. A family sitting room.
Laundry.

**Cars.** Three on the setback by the service street. No garage room.

**Wishes.** Bedrooms away from both streets. The diwaniya's guests
never see the family. Kitchen next to dining and near the diwaniya
kitchen. The parents' bedroom gets the morning sun. The stair must
reach a second floor later.

**Limits.** Budget 320,000 KD for the shell. Municipality ratio and
setbacks as they stand today.

## What the run must record

- How the architect enters big, medium and small (the screen takes
  numbers today).
- Where the three cars go with no garage room (the frontage claim
  knows bays; a bay on the setback is not yet a thing without a room).
- How the second floor "later" is handled (add it as a normal storey;
  P8 waits on what the run shows).
- A dressing room per master suite (not in the room-type table).
