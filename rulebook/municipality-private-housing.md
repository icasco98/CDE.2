# Kuwait Municipality: private and model housing

**Source.** Kuwait Municipality, "Requirements and specifications for
model and private housing buildings", as amended by Ministerial
Resolution 451/2016. Retrieved from baladia.gov.kw (construction
system, page 3) on 10 September 2026 by the owner.

**Status.** Every rule below is a **wall** (hard constraint) and is
`sourced`. By the owner's decision these are the rules the tool
follows until the Municipality's own website publishes a change; the
owner supplies the new text when that happens, and the entry is
updated with a new retrieval date. News reports of other figures are
not a source.

Numbers were transcribed from a printout. Each one must be checked
against the page by the owner before it is marked verified.

## Building ratio (البند الأول)

| Plot area | Allowed gross floor area |
|---|---|
| 401 m² and above | 210% of plot area |
| 350 to 400 m² | 210% of plot area + 120 m² |
| 250 to 349 m² | 800 m² total, subject to floors and setbacks |

- Distributed over three floors including roof annexes.
- Basement is not counted and may cover the whole plot.
- Ground, first and second floors may have different areas as the
  design requires, within setbacks and the total.
- Roof-level construction on the second-floor roof up to 100 m²,
  counted in the total, not overlooking the main or service street;
  a stair house up to 25 m² is not counted.
- A lift for a person with a disability in the main building is not
  counted, subject to setbacks.

## Setbacks (البند الثاني)

Plots under 750 m²:

- Main building at least 2 m from the boundary on the service street.
- At least 1.5 m from every other boundary.
- Sight-angle setback at least 1.5 m.

Plots of 750 m² and above:

- At least 3 m from the service-street boundary.
- At least 2 m from every other boundary.
- A plot whose service-street frontage is 20 m or less may use 1.5 m
  toward neighbours and other streets and squares, not the service
  street.
- Sight-angle setback at least 2.0 m.

Building on the boundary:

- Ground floor only, on every side except the service street, where
  it may occupy up to 50% of the frontage and at most 15 m; parts on
  the boundary no higher than 5 m above the average kerb of the
  highest internal street, plus a parapet of at most 50 cm.
- Upper floors never on the boundary, with three exceptions: houses
  originally built attached in government housing schemes; owners
  with a mutual, registered agreement licensing at the same time; and
  plots of 365 m² or less being demolished and rebuilt, which may
  attach toward neighbours, back streets, pedestrian paths and
  squares up to 50% of the street frontage, the unattached parts
  keeping 1.5 m from the service street. Attached facades carry no
  windows, balconies or air-conditioning units toward the neighbour.

## Heights (البند الثالث)

- Maximum building height 15 m from the average kerb level of the
  highest internal street to the roof of the second floor; at most
  three floors including the ground floor.
- Minimum clear floor height 3 m; internal floor heights are free
  within the 15 m.
- Basement at most 1.80 m above the average regulatory level of the
  plot; minimum clear basement height 2.75 m.
- Stair and lift rooms may exceed the maximum by up to 3 m.
- Roof parapet of the main building at most 2 m.
- Boundary wall between 0.50 m and 3 m. On a plot with more than one
  frontage, no gate within 5 m of the shared corner where two plots
  meet at 90°.

## Minimum room sizes (البند الرابع)

| Space | Minimum area | Minimum width |
|---|---|---|
| Room | 10 m² | 3 m |
| Kitchen | 7.5 m² | 2 m |
| Bathroom | 4 m² | 1.75 m |
| WC | 1.5 m² | 1.20 m |
| Corridor inside the unit | – | 1.20 m clear |

## Stairs (البند الخامس)

- More than one stair is allowed (one formal, one service) provided a
  floor cannot be split into more than one dwelling.
- Main stair and landing at least 1.20 m clear; landing width equals
  stair width.
- Twice the riser plus the tread between 60 and 65 cm; riser at most
  17 cm.
- Headroom at least 2.20 m.
- At most 14 risers in one straight run, unless the building or the
  stair serves one floor only.
- Natural light and ventilation required.

## Projections (البند السادس)

- On facades built to the boundary: fins, windows, cornices and the
  like at most 50 cm; the top roof cornice at most 1 m; projections
  start at 2.20 m above ground.
- Inside setbacks the same limits apply and projections are not
  counted when measuring the setback.

## Light wells (البند السابع)

- Serving rooms: shortest side at least a quarter of the height
  served, never under 1.5 m; area at least 9 m².
- Serving kitchens and stairs: shortest side at least a tenth of the
  height served, never under 1.20 m; area at least 4 m².
- Serving bathrooms and WCs: shortest side at least 1 m, area at
  least 2 m², unless mechanically ventilated.
- Enclosed wells need floor access and fire protection per the fire
  department.

## Pergolas and canopies (البند الثامن)

- Allowed around the building and on the roof, no higher than the
  stair house, counted in the ratio.
- A pergola or canopy over the stair house for water tanks up to
  1.80 m is not counted.
- An entrance canopy from the main building to the gate is not
  counted if no wider than the entrance, at most 3 m, open at the
  sides.

## Detached annexes (البند التاسع)

- At most 150 m² and at most 20% of the plot, within the ratio.
- On any street, at most 50% of that frontage and at most 15 m.
- Roof no higher than 5 m from the kerb, even with a basement.
- Building above an existing annex must join the main building as
  one homogeneous structure, within setbacks, with structural proof.

## Basement (البند العاشر)

- May cover the whole plot; a sunken courtyard without setback is
  allowed with shoring toward the service street.
- May hold halls, rooms, parking and services such as kitchen and
  bathrooms, with natural light, ventilation and drainage.
- Parts may be open to the sky.

## Building services (البند الحادي عشر)

Water tanks, antennas and air-conditioning units in a suitable place
under the conditions set for them.
